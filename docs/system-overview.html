Context

Why We Built This

  • Marketers take half a day to draft a single campaign emailer by hand
  • Each business unit has its own identity — brand drift is constant when humans copy-paste templates
  • HTML email is unforgiving — Outlook breaks what Gmail renders, and we find out only after sending
  • Engineering on every campaign doesn't scale — we are the bottleneck

"An emailer should take a marketer five minutes, not five hours — and it should never break in Outlook."

Under the Hood

Self-Hosted, Single-VM

  • Frontend — React SPA served as static files by nginx:alpine
  • Backend — FastAPI (Python 3.12) with embedded MJML renderer
  • Database — MySQL 8 on a private docker network, never exposed to the internet
  • AI — Anthropic Claude (Sonnet for generation, Haiku for refinement) with prompt caching
  • Edge — Nginx Proxy Manager on Digital Ocean handles TLS, tunnelling into the NAS over Wireguard

Why It Matters

Every email draft, asset, and brand kit lives on infrastructure we control — no third-party SaaS holding marketing data.

One docker compose up brings up the entire stack from source. No container registry, no CI dependency, full audit of what is running.

Database persists in a named volume; rebuilds never lose data.

Brand Safety

Brand Drift, Solved Structurally

Brand kits are not guidelines — they are inputs to every AI call.

  • Each emailer locks to a brand kit at creation — no half-applied identity
  • The brand kit (colors, fonts, tone, disclaimer) is injected into every prompt — the model literally cannot generate off-brand
  • Admin-only CRUD for brand kits — marketing onboards new business units without engineering
  • Logo and assets resolve through our backend — no broken images in delivered emails

If you can't tell which business unit sent it from the colors alone, the brand kit failed. We don't let that happen.
